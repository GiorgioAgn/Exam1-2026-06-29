Overview

This scenic loop starts in the valley and climbs through mixed woodland to an open ridge with wide views. Expect some rocky sections and a short scramble near the summit.

Route details

  • Distance: 8 km
  • Ascent: 450 m
  • Estimated time: 3–4 hours
